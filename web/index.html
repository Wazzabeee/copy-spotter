copy-spotter

Find copied passages between documents and see them side by side.

Your files never leave your device. They are read and compared inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, and this page has no analytics or trackers. The first visit downloads the comparison program (about 20 MB), then your browser keeps it.
